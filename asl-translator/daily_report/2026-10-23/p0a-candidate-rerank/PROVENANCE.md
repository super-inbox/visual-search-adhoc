## Provenance gaps

1. **The displayed video is not the official input.** For R15, R40, R42 (focus) and R45, the official window barely
   overlaps or does not overlap the video on the page (IoU ≤ 0.02). Across all 45, 9 have IoU 0 and 23 have
   IoU < 0.5. Visual claims about the "official" output cannot rest on the page video. Human P0-A review is
   text-vs-reference and must not cite the page video for D-A.
2. **Official crop RGB video is not stored locally.** Only the official poses are stored. A matched D-A video can be
   cut from `p018-work/downloads/test_raw_videos.zip` using the realigned times, but full-frame, not the official
   crop.
3. **More than 256 frames:** R03, R04 (A only), R10, R11, R12, R14, R19, R20, R21, R23, R36, R38, R39, R44. Uni-Sign
   subsamples these at random, so the frozen per-clip seed rule (protocol §1.3) is required. Ordinary control R20
   and R04 (D-A) are affected.
4. **Fp16 vs fp32.** The stored P0-12 texts are T4/fp16. Today's decode is CPU/fp32, so T may differ from the
   inspection-sheet text. That comparison is descriptive, and T (today's CPU output) is the control.
5. **P0-15 split overlap.** 30/45 cases (including R15, R40, R41) were in the P0-15 "final" split. That split is
   already retired from confirmatory use. This is fine for diagnosis and is recorded here.
6. **Probe scores are domain-specific.** The P0-14 out-of-fold mT5 probe scores were computed on official-pose
   features, so R2 runs in D-A only. Mapping clip IDs to rows uses the P0-14 `splits/manifest.csv` test_clean
   order and must be asserted at decode time.
