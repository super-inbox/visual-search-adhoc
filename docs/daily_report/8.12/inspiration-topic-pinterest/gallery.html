Interpretation

  • Pinterest is supplemental, not a replacement for Google, based on this pilot — it never matched Google/Behance's 100% outbound-URL and source-attribution completeness, and its usable-candidate rate swung from 0/5 to 5/5 depending on query.
  • Production integration into Search, Creative Exploration, Inspiration Hub, or Topic Pages was NOT performed here. This is data preparation and comparison only.
  • This is an adhoc research visualization built from the CSVs and local thumbnails already captured in this task — no new data was collected to produce this page, and no missing images or metadata were invented.
  • Next step per the daily report: get product confirmation on the 4 needs_product_confirmation mappings and a decision on the 3 missing topics (figure, business card, certificate).
