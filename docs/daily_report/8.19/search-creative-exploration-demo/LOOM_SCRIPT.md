# Loom Narration Script — Search + Creative Exploration

Matches `DEMO_PLAN.md`'s click sequence exactly. Keep it conversational — read it out loud once
before recording and trim anything that feels stiff. Target: **60–90 seconds of narration**,
timed to the on-screen actions, not a separate voiceover written to be read in full regardless of
pace.

Tone note: this is for the boss — product-oriented and concrete ("here's what it does"), not
research-oriented ("here's what we tested"). Save the caveats and evidence trail for
`DEMO_PLAN.md`; keep this script focused on the product moment.

---

## 1. User intent / query (~10s)

> "Say I'm about to launch a new tea brand and I want a quick gut-check on what's already out
> there — then I want to turn that into actual creative directions fast. Let's start with
> search."

*[Screen: Curify homepage, `curify-ai.com`]*

---

## 2. Search / inspiration discovery (~20s)

*[Type into the search bar: "modern tea brand identity" → Enter]*

> "I'll search 'modern tea brand identity.'"

*[Results load — top row shows the three "Tea Brand" cards]*

> "Right away I'm seeing real tea-brand examples — contemporary, earthy-elegant, a more heritage
> take. That's a fast read on the visual landscape before I've designed anything myself."

*(If the "Few results" banner is visible, just keep talking over the results — don't call it
out.)*

---

## 3. Transition into Creative Exploration (~10s)

> "Now, that's search helping me look around. To actually generate creative directions for my
> own brand, I'll switch over to our Creative Exploration tool — Brand Direction Explorer."

*[Navigate to `/tools/brand-direction-explorer`, click the "Chinese tea brand style exploration"
tab]*

*(Say this plainly — it's a manual switch to a separate tool, not a magic handoff, and that's
fine. Don't imply the search click carried anything over.)*

---

## 4. Generation / directions (~35s)

*[Fill in the brief: brand name "山岚茶事," product type "Modern Chinese tea," the description,
applications, and desired tone from `DEMO_PLAN.md` §3]*

> "I give it one brief — brand name, what it is, where it'll show up, the tone I want — and
> that's it. No prompt-writing."

*[Wait for the three direction cards to render]*

> "And in a few seconds I get three genuinely different directions from that one brief: a modern
> minimalist take, a cultural-contemporary take, and a calmer, more zen read. Three real options,
> not three versions of the same idea."

*[Click "Cultural Contemporary" to select it]*

> "I'll go with 'Cultural Contemporary' — it's the closest match to that earthy, modern-heritage
> style I liked in search."

*[Click "Generate visual"]*

> "One click, and it builds the actual visual from that direction."

*[Wait for / show the final image — or the fallback still, narrated per `DEMO_PLAN.md` §6]*

---

## 5. Final takeaway (~10s)

> "So: search to get inspired, Creative Exploration to turn a one-line brief into real, distinct
> creative directions, and a finished visual at the end. That's the whole loop."

*[Hold on the final image, cut.]*

---

## Notes for whoever records this

- If you end up on the fallback path (Stage-1 or Stage-2 didn't come through live), just adjust
  the tense naturally — e.g. "and here's what that direction produces" over the archived still,
  rather than forcing the scripted line to fit a screen that isn't actually updating live.
- Don't ad-lib a claim that search results feed the generator — re-read `DEMO_PLAN.md` §2 if
  you're unsure how to phrase the transition.
- If you swap to the "Brand Design" topic-tab fallback for step 2 instead of the typed search,
  the intent line still works — just say "let's browse Brand Design" instead of "let's search."
