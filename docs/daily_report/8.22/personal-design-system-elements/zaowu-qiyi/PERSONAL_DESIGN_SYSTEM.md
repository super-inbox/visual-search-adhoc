## 1. Visual DNA

**Keywords:** super-symbol-led · one-hue-plus-accent · bilingual · CGI-staged · category-adaptive ·
systemic (not one-off).

**Atmosphere:** confident, commercial, symbol-first packaging design that moves fluently between a
ceremonial/heritage register and a playful/contemporary one depending on category, while keeping
one constant method underneath: reduce the brand to one repeatable mark, then apply it consistently
across every format. [A]/[B], all 10 works.

## 2. Color System

- **One dominant hue family per brand, one small saturated accent** — not a multicolor palette.
  [A] work_01 (gold/maroon+red), 02 (mint+coral/blue), 03 (black/grey+blue), 06 (burgundy+gold), 07
  (periwinkle+pink/green).
- **Saturation/contrast**: ranges from near-monochrome+one-accent (work_03) to richly layered
  warm-on-warm (work_01, 06) — both follow the same one-dominant-plus-accent structure. [B]
- **Warm/cool split tracks category**: warm/dark for heritage-premium (01, 06), cool/pastel for
  soft-goods/wellness (02, 04, 08, 10). [A]/[B]
- Palette examples below are **approximate visual reads from screenshots**, not claimed production
  colors — see `PORTFOLIO_SOURCE.md` (8.21) for the standing policy on this.

## 3. Typography

- **Bilingual Chinese+Latin pairing recurs systematically**, but hierarchy (which language leads in
  size) is decided per brief, not fixed. [A] work_01 (large 摘要 + small ZHAI YAO), work_03
  (oversized artevive + small 文创芝), work_09 (近-parity bilingual per-SKU names).
- **Display type recedes when an illustrated motif is the hero** (work_01's dragon, work_09's
  mascot); it dominates when the mark itself is more abstract/geometric (work_03). [B]
- No source typeface is claimed or identifiable at this evidence resolution. [C]

## 4. Composition & Layout

- **Two staging modes**: dramatic asymmetric/diagonal on dark grounds (work_01, 03, 05, 06) vs.
  cleaner, more centered on light/pastel grounds (work_02, 04, 07, 08, 10). [A]
- **Ensemble composition for multi-SKU/multi-piece lines** (work_05's hero+lineup, work_06's
  gift-set flat-lay, work_09's four-format lineup) vs. single-hero shots for single-SKU works. [A]
- **Front-panel hierarchy**: graphic device first, color field second, wordmark/utility text last,
  consistently. [B]

## 5. Shape Language

- **Circle/roundel recurs as a logo-container shape** across otherwise unrelated brands. [A]
  work_01 (small red circle in wordmark), 05 (circular "©CA" mark), 06 (circular floral roundel),
  09 (circular leaf/arrow mark).
- No repeated border/frame device was found — do not assume one. [C, absence noted]

## 6. Graphic Devices / Motifs (Super-Symbol Methodology)

- **One fixed, simplified mark is reused verbatim across every SKU/format in a family**, at large,
  central placement — the studio's own stated "超级符号" method. [A] work_05 (identical mark on 3
  SKU colors), work_06 (roundel repeats on ≥2 pieces), work_09 (mark + mascot span 4 formats).
- Symbol style is brief-specific: **ornamental linework** for heritage (work_01's dragon) vs.
  **flat-abstract geometry** for modern brand-symbol work (work_03) vs. **named mascot character**
  for contemporary snacks (work_09's grandpa figure). [B]
- Where checkable, the symbol **encodes product/brand meaning**, not decoration: work_01's dragon +
  book-shaped bottle for "摘要" (excerpt), work_07's cat-paw for a cat-paw-named product, work_09's
  grandpa character for "Hawthorn Grandpa." [A], 3 works.

## 7. Imagery / Illustration

- **Photoreal CGI-rendered mockup is the default** (7 of 10 works: 01, 03, 04, 05, 06, 07, 09), with
  **real environmental/lifestyle photography as an occasional, deliberate exception** (work_02,
  08). [A]
- Illustration, where present, is flat continuous linework (work_01) or a folk-art character style
  (work_09) — not painterly/textured rendering. [B]

## 8. Material / Texture

Screenshot-resolution single images cannot responsibly support material claims (paper grain,
foil, matte/gloss finish) beyond what's visually obvious. [C] — a kraft/uncoated cardboard
substrate is visible in work_07 (natural brown box texture, distinct from the glossy-coated boxes
elsewhere), the one material observation confident enough to record.

## 10. Design Personality

Confident and legible over minimal; culturally rooted when the brief calls for it (heritage/
gifting categories), playful/contemporary otherwise; systems-minded — the studio designs one
reusable device per brand rather than a single hero image. [B], cross-checked across 10 works.

## 11. Do / Don't

**Do:** one dominant hue + one accent; one fixed mark reused everywhere; bilingual naming; CGI
staging by default; match backdrop to category; ensemble shots for multi-SKU lines.

**Don't:** two dominant colors on one item; a unique mark per SKU; assume one language always
leads; assume one illustration style fits every brand; assume a border/frame device (none found);
invent exact colors/fonts.
