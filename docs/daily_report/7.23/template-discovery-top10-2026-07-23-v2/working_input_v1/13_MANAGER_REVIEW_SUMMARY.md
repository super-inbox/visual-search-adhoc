## Why 10, not more
The prior session's evidence review converged on exactly 10 candidates that survive the "zero/low results at
every historical timepoint, not just the latest run" test. Several other queries in the same benchmark
(diploma, puzzle-EN, 优惠券, 笔袋, map, flashcard, vocabulary card, 单词卡, 会员卡, 礼品卡, character) were
considered and explicitly excluded — either because they are search regressions (content used to exist and was
lost) or because intent is too ambiguous to define a single scalable template. See
`07_SEARCH_VS_CONTENT_GAP_CLASSIFICATION.csv` for the full 31-query classification.

## Key risk to flag
Six of the ten candidates (T01, T02, T05, T08, T09, T10) have **no competitor screenshot on file** — their
evidence rests on internal benchmark data and the 7/17 Curify live screenshots only. This does not weaken the
content-gap finding (Curify's own search results are the primary evidence), but it means these six cannot yet
be cross-validated against what competitors are shipping. See `11_MISSING_IMAGE_EVIDENCE.md`.
