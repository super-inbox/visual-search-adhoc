## Decisions with no direct rule mapping (flagged, not hidden)

- **The specific flavor choices** (Lychee Rose, Cucumber Mint, Yuzu Ginger) are brief-level product
  decisions, not derived from the Personal Design System — flagged here so it's clear they are
  brief-authoring choices, not portfolio-traced ones.
- **The specific brand name and its "泉/荔" wordplay** are brief-level, not system-derived.
- **The "spark white" accent choice specifically being white-gold** (as opposed to any other single
  accent hue) is a new creative decision within Rule 1's constraint (one small accent), not itself
  derived from any work's specific accent color.
