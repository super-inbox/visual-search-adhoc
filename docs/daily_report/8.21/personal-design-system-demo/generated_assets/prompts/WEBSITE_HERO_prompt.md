# WEBSITE_HERO — generation record

## Method actually used
`.dc.html` Design Component. Source: `canvas_src/WebsiteHero.dc.html`, importing `Can.dc.html`
three times (full family, smaller scale) and one `SproutBubbleSymbol.dc.html` instance (small
corner brand mark beside the wordmark).

## Photoreal generation prompt (for future use with an actual image-gen tool)

From `ASSET_PLAN.md` §Asset 5:

> three 330ml cans (blush/coral, mid-green, pale gold) with identical white-gold bubble-sprout
> marks, arranged on a simple light-toned shelf/display fixture or within a wide bright
> website-hero crop, bright even lighting, generous negative space reserved for text overlay,
> photoreal CGI render consistent with Assets 1-2.

## What was actually generated instead

A wide-format banner: a left copy column (brand mark, bilingual headline "轻盈气泡 / 源头养分" +
"LICHUAN SPARKLING", and a "立即选购 · Shop Now" CTA pill) and a right product column showing all
three SKU cans at reduced scale, evenly lit on the same bright background used across the other
assets. This fulfils the "website hero" brief (concise bilingual copy + CTA + product family) as a
flat/vector composition rather than a photoreal render with a physical shelf fixture — the shelf/
retail-display framing named in the brief was simplified to a clean web-banner layout, which is
flagged as a scope simplification in `ASSET_GENERATION_NOTES.md`.
