# Brand Brief — 荔泉气泡 (Lichuan Sparkling)

This is a newly authored brief for Phase 3's generalization test. It is not derived from, and does
not describe, any project in the source portfolio (`PORTFOLIO_SOURCE.md`) — no sampled work is a
beverage, and none is a canned/carbonated product.

## Brand name

**荔泉气泡 / Lichuan Sparkling**. "泉" (spring) anchors the brand in a clean, natural water
source; "荔" carries a light fruit-freshness association (echoed directly in one of the three
launch flavors) without committing the whole line to being a lychee product; "气泡" (sparkling/
bubbles) names the category directly.

## Category

Plant-protein sparkling water — a lightly carbonated, plant-based-protein-fortified water,
positioned as a "healthy indulgence" alternative to soda, not a juice or traditional soft drink.

## Target audience

Young urban professionals (roughly 24-35) in first-tier Chinese cities — desk-based, health-aware,
time-poor, willing to pay a premium for a drink that feels considered rather than default.

## Positioning

"The sparkling water that still does something for you." Refreshment with a functional plant-
protein benefit, positioned above mass-market flavored sparkling water and below premium
wellness-tonic pricing — an everyday-premium desk/commute drink, not an occasional treat.

## Consumer occasion

Mid-morning or mid-afternoon desk break; post-workout light refreshment; a "better than a second
coffee" pick-me-up; grab-and-go from a convenience fridge or office pantry.

## Personality

Clear-headed, quietly confident, unfussy — refreshing rather than indulgent, modern rather than
nostalgic. Deliberately lighter and more contemporary in tone than the source portfolio's heritage/
ceremonial register (see `PERSONAL_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` §2, §8) — this brand sits in the studio's
"contemporary/everyday" register, not its "heritage/premium" one.

## Differentiation

Most competing sparkling waters lead with fruit-flavor imagery alone; 荔泉气泡 leads with one
consistent, simplified graphic device across every flavor (see Super-Symbol in
`BRAND_VISUAL_SYSTEM.md`) so the brand is recognizable at a glance across a full shelf set, rather
than relying on flavor-photography alone to carry shelf presence.

## Packaging format

Standard 330ml slim aluminum can, matte-finish print wrap — a realistic, manufacturable baseline
(see `BRAND_VISUAL_SYSTEM.md` §Packaging/Form for why no custom can silhouette is required).

## Core message

"轻盈气泡，源头养分" ("Light bubbles, real nourishment") — sparkling refreshment that doesn't ask
you to trade taste for benefit.

## Required asset touchpoints

Five Phase 4 deliverables (full detail in `ASSET_PLAN.md`): a hero/key visual, a 3-SKU packaging
family shot, one social media campaign post, one campaign poster, and one website-hero-or-retail-
display asset.
