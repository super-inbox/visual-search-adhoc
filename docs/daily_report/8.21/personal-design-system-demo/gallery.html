Plant-protein sparkling water · young Chinese urban professionals

8 Limitation & Next Step

Current assets are flat/vector proxies, not photoreal CGI. No photoreal image-generation tool was available in this environment — every PNG in Section 5 is a browser screenshot of a rendered .dc.html canvas (CSS gradients + SVG shapes standing in for a can), not a diffusion-model or photographic render. SOCIAL_POST specifically substitutes a flat illustration for the brief's requested real lifestyle photography. This is a disclosed capability gap, not a claim of production readiness.

Next step: connect the already-saved, fully-specified system and per-asset generation prompts (generated_assets/prompts/*.md) to an actual photoreal image-generation model. Every step upstream of final rendering — portfolio evidence extraction, rule synthesis, brand brief authoring, and asset prompt construction — is already complete and automated; only the final rendering step needs a real model wired in. Full accounting: LIMITATIONS_AND_NEXT_STEPS.md, FINAL_DEMO_VERDICT.md.
