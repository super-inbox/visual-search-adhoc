# Asset Plan — 荔泉气泡 (Phase 4 scope, not generated yet)

Five assets, each specified against `BRAND_VISUAL_SYSTEM.md`. No images are generated in this
phase — this document defines what Phase 4 should produce and how to judge it.

---

## Asset 1 — Hero product / brand key visual

**Purpose**: the single image most people will see first — establishes the super-symbol, the
brand's typographic hierarchy, and the CGI-rendering default in one shot.

**Composition**: a single Lychee Rose can, centered, three-quarter angle, on a bright near-white
ground with soft directional studio lighting and visible condensation droplets. Generous whitespace
around the can (per §Composition, moderate-to-generous density).

**Copy**: "荔泉气泡" (large) / "LICHUAN SPARKLING" (small, beneath) / "荔枝玫瑰 · Lychee Rose"
(smallest, on-can only — no additional headline copy needed for this asset).

**System rules used**: Rule 2 (symbol placement/scale), Rule 3 (bilingual hierarchy), Rule 5 (CGI
render default), Rule 6 (light backdrop branch).

**Visual consistency requirements**: super-symbol must match the exact geometry described in
`BRAND_VISUAL_SYSTEM.md` §Super-Symbol (bubble+sprout, spark-white fill, circle container); no
second dominant color introduced; lighting must be bright/cool, not dark/dramatic.

**Generation prompt ingredients**: 330ml matte aluminum can, soft blush/coral dominant field,
white-gold bubble-sprout mark in a thin circle outline centered upper-third of can face, condensation
droplets, bright near-white studio background, soft single-source cool lighting, photoreal CGI
product render, three-quarter hero angle, generous negative space.

---

## Asset 2 — 3-SKU packaging family shot

**Purpose**: demonstrates the family/ensemble system — the mechanism by which the three flavors
read as one brand while remaining shelf-distinguishable.

**Composition**: all three cans (Lychee Rose, Cucumber Mint, Yuzu Ginger) arranged in a shallow
row or gentle arc, evenly lit, same camera height and angle for all three — no single can staged as
more "hero" than the others in this specific asset (that role belongs to Asset 1).

**Copy**: brand wordmark may appear once, small, at the edge of the frame (not repeated three
times) — flavor names appear only on-can.

**System rules used**: Rule 7 (ensemble/family composition), Rule 1 (per-SKU dominant hue + shared
accent), Rule 2 (identical mark across all three).

**Visual consistency requirements**: identical can silhouette, identical symbol scale/position/fill
across all three; only the dominant background hue and the small quaternary flavor icon differ
between cans, exactly as specified in the SKU table.

**Generation prompt ingredients**: three 330ml cans side by side (blush/coral, mid-green, pale
gold), identical white-gold bubble-sprout circular mark at identical scale/position on each,
identical typographic stack on each, bright even studio lighting, near-white background, photoreal
CGI render, condensation on all three, evenly spaced flat composition.

---

## Asset 3 — Social media campaign post

**Purpose**: a square/vertical social-first asset introducing the brand with a lifestyle moment,
using photography as the deliberate secondary mode (per Rule 5).

**Composition**: real (or real-styled) lifestyle photography — a hand holding a single can at a
desk or during a commute pause, shallow depth of field, one can in focus. Overlaid brand mark and a
short campaign line, not a full retail label re-creation.

**Copy**: headline "轻盈气泡，源头养分" ("Light bubbles, real nourishment"); small overlay of the
brand wordmark and the sprout-bubble mark (as a graphic overlay, not necessarily the physical can
label at this crop).

**System rules used**: Rule 5 (photography as valid secondary mode, explicitly reserved for
social/poster per `BRAND_VISUAL_SYSTEM.md`), Rule 2 (mark reused as a graphic overlay even outside
packaging).

**Visual consistency requirements**: the overlaid sprout-bubble mark must match Asset 1/2's
geometry exactly; the dominant hue of whichever can appears should be one of the three defined SKU
hues, not a new color; no second dominant color introduced in the overlay/graphic treatment.

**Generation prompt ingredients**: candid desk/commute lifestyle photo style, soft natural light,
shallow depth of field, one 荔泉气泡 can in focus (any single SKU), short Chinese headline overlay,
small white-gold sprout-bubble mark as a corner or footer graphic, minimal additional text.

## Asset 4 — Campaign poster

**Purpose**: a larger-format brand-building poster (print or digital out-of-home), establishing
brand personality beyond the product shot alone.

**Composition**: a bolder, more graphic treatment than Asset 1 — the super-symbol enlarged as a
dominant background graphic device (e.g. a large, softly-rendered bubble-sprout shape occupying a
significant portion of the poster field), with one hero can placed in front of it at a smaller
scale, plus the core message as a headline.

**Copy**: headline "轻盈气泡，源头养分"; subhead "LICHUAN SPARKLING"; no flavor-specific copy (this
poster represents the brand, not one SKU).

**System rules used**: Rule 2 (symbol as the dominant device, here scaled up beyond its usual
on-can size specifically for this poster context — an allowed extension since the rule specifies
consistent *shape*, not a fixed absolute size across every touchpoint), Rule 5 (CGI-rendered can
kept consistent even as the surrounding graphic treatment becomes bolder), Rule 6 (light backdrop
branch retained).

**Visual consistency requirements**: the enlarged background symbol must be the *exact same shape*
as the on-can mark (no redesign for this asset); the featured can (if any single SKU is chosen) must
match its defined SKU spec exactly.

**Generation prompt ingredients**: large soft white-gold bubble-sprout silhouette as a background
graphic on a bright pale ground, one 330ml can (any SKU) placed in front at moderate scale with
condensation, bold but clean Chinese headline typography, small English subhead, generous
whitespace around the composition, poster/out-of-home aspect ratio.

## Asset 5 — Website hero / merch & retail display

**Purpose**: a wide-format asset for either a website hero banner or a retail shelf/display unit —
functions as the brand's "at scale" presence in a commercial environment.

**Composition**: the 3-SKU family (as in Asset 2) staged within or in front of a simple retail
display/shelf unit context, OR a wide website-hero crop with the family shot plus generous
left/right whitespace for UI text overlay (choose whichever the actual Phase 4 channel requires —
this spec covers both since the visual requirements are the same).

**Copy**: brand wordmark and core message may appear as UI overlay text (website) or as
display-unit signage graphics (retail) — not printed onto the cans themselves beyond their existing
on-can copy.

**System rules used**: Rule 7 (ensemble composition), Rule 1/2 (per-can consistency, unchanged from
Asset 2), Rule 6 (light backdrop retained even in a retail-display context — no dark/dramatic
staging introduced for this touchpoint).

**Visual consistency requirements**: cans must be pixel-consistent with Asset 2 in symbol, hue, and
hierarchy — this asset should look like the same three products as Asset 2, staged in a different
context, not redesigned for the occasion.

**Generation prompt ingredients**: three 330ml cans (blush/coral, mid-green, pale gold) with
identical white-gold bubble-sprout marks, arranged on a simple light-toned shelf/display fixture or
within a wide bright website-hero crop, bright even lighting, generous negative space reserved for
text overlay, photoreal CGI render consistent with Assets 1-2.

---

## Cross-asset consistency note for Phase 4

Every asset above reuses the same fixed elements (super-symbol geometry, the three SKU hues, the
typographic hierarchy, and the light/bright staging register) and varies only composition, copy
emphasis, and whether real photography or CGI rendering is used — exactly the discipline
`BRAND_VISUAL_SYSTEM.md` and `BRAND_SYSTEM_CONSISTENCY_CHECK.md` establish. Phase 4 should treat
Assets 1 and 2 as the canonical reference for the mark/color/type system, and check Assets 3-5
against them rather than re-deriving the system independently per asset.
